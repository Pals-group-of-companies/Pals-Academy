Where little learners feel at home.

Pals Academy is a kindergarten in Waithaka offering Baby Class, PP1 and PP2: a joyful, caring start to every child’s school journey.

Our story

[How Pals Academy started: who founded it, when, and why.]

Today we focus on what matters most in the early years: helping every child feel safe, loved and excited to learn. Small classes mean teachers know each child well, and our days mix learning with play, music, art and time outdoors.

Pals Academy is part of the Pals Group, and our school transport is run by our sister company, Pals Travel & Leisure.

A word from our Head Teacher

Welcome to Pals Academy. The first years of school shape how a child feels about learning for the rest of their life, and we take that seriously.

Here, every child is greeted by name, encouraged to ask questions and given the time to grow at their own pace. We play, we sing, we read and we count, and along the way our little ones become confident, kind and curious.

Our door is always open. Come and visit us, meet our teachers and see the joy in our classrooms for yourself.

Mr MbwayaHead Teacher, Pals Academy
