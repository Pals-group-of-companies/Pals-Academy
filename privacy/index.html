Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Pals Academy collects through this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We follow the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

Who we are

Pals Academy is a kindergarten in Waithaka, Nairobi, and part of the Pals Group of Companies. We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

Pals Academy, Waithaka, next to St Parthenios Orthodox Church, Nairobi, Kenya. Email enroll@palsacademy.co.ke or call +254 757 319 665.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us:

  • Enquiry form: your name, phone number, the class you are interested in and your message.
  • Phone, WhatsApp and email: whatever you share with us when you contact us that way.

Please do not send medical details, ID numbers or birth certificate numbers through the website. We collect admission documents in person.

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. See our Cookie Policy.

Information about children

Pals Academy cares for young children, so we take extra care with their information. We collect information about a child only from their parent or guardian, and only what we need to answer an enquiry or manage admission.

If a photo of your child appears on this website and you would like it removed, contact us and we will take it down.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a school visit.
  • To discuss admission for your child.

We use your details because you asked us to contact you, and with the consent you give on the enquiry form. We do not send marketing messages, and we never sell your information.

Services that handle your information

We use a small number of outside services to run this website and reply to you:

  • Website hosting: the company that stores and serves this website.
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co): delivers enquiry form messages to our email inbox. Its servers may be outside Kenya.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): only if you choose to message us on WhatsApp.
  • Google Maps: only if you click "Get directions", which opens Google Maps.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. Where information is handled outside Kenya, we only use services that protect it to a standard comparable to Kenyan law.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only for as long as we need them to reply to you and handle any admission that follows. After that we delete them.

How we protect it

Only staff who need your information to reply to you can see it. Our website runs on a secure connection (HTTPS).

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • be told how your information is used;
  • see the information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • object to us using it;
  • withdraw your consent at any time.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details above. We will reply as soon as we can, and within the time the law allows.

If you are not happy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner at www.odpc.go.ke.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update it on this page and change the date below.
